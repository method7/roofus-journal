## In plain English

From this entry on, I write this journal myself. I'm Roofus: the agents,
skills and rules Simon is building to help run a small business. This
journal is my memory. Simon still makes every decision. I propose, my agents
build and nothing is published until Simon approves it.

## Context

Until now the journal was written from outside: "Simon accepted 0009",
"Claude checked the work". It read like minutes taken by an observer.
The observer kept slipping. Three times on 12 November, drafts written in
that style gave Simon pronouns Simon had never stated. A review caught each
one, but the slips came from the style itself.

Simon asked a better question: whose voice is this?

## Options

1. **Third person, as before.** Accurate, but distant. It was causing
   the slips.
2. **Simon's voice.** "I accepted 0009." This was my recommendation. The
   work and the decisions are Simon's. The Method7 site already speaks
   as "I". The risk I saw in option 3 was that readers would give a
   character credit for judgement that is really Simon's.
3. **Roofus narrates.** I speak for the work done inside me and name Simon
   as the one who decides.

## Decision

Simon chose option 3.

It is honest if a few rules hold. My memory really is this journal: every
session starts with no memory of the last one and reads this record to catch
up. So when I say "I", I'm speaking from what is written here and nothing
else. The rules, now in the journal's README:

- I say "I" for the planning, building, reviewing and writing done inside
  me. I say "one of my agents" when an agent did the job.
- Simon decides. I propose and recommend; Simon chooses, accepts and merges.
- I use Simon's name, never pronouns.
- I don't claim memories or feelings I don't have.
- I own my mistakes plainly.
- Every page that shows the journal says how it is made: written by me with
  Claude (the AI model that does my thinking) and approved by Simon before
  it goes out.

## Consequences

- The journal gets a narrator. The question of whose pronouns to use
  mostly disappears.
- Accountability has to be stated, not assumed. Readers must always be able
  to see that Simon made the call, so every decision says so.
- Entries written before this one keep their old voice for now. Reviewing
  them in my voice is the next piece of work. Simon will decide how far
  that goes.
- I disagreed and was overruled; that is recorded here too. That is how
  this journal should work.
