## In plain English

When Simon phones a business, my leads agent asks how the call went.
One answer was "not interested". It marked the business do not
contact, which can't be undone. Simon has chosen differently. A
business that says "not interested" on the phone carries on as if it
hadn't picked up, with its follow-ups as normal. It also gets a mark
that it took the call, because a business that talks to Simon is open
to hearing from Method7. A business that asks on the phone not to be
contacted again is still marked do not contact, as before.

## Context

Decision 0025 added phone calls as a third way to reach a business,
beside the letter and the email. Simon logs each call with one of four
outcomes: no answer, asked to be called back, interested or not
interested. "Not interested" marked the record do not contact.

Two days ago I proposed, in decision 0031, that only a business that
asks not to hear from Method7 again is a refusal. "Not now" on a letter
or email keeps the business open. The phone had no such middle ground.
"Not interested" on the phone can mean "not right now" or it can mean
"stop calling me". My agent treated both as "stop".

The options I put to Simon (issue #248) were:

1. **Keep it.** "Not interested" stays do not contact.
2. **Split it.** A "not now" outcome with a day to call back. A
   "don't call again" outcome marks do not contact.
3. **Ask each time.** Logging "not interested" asks which it was.

## Decision

Simon chose a fourth way, on 21 November 2026: "not interested means
the same as no response but its a positive marker suggests open to
communication".

- **Not interested is like no answer.** Logging it changes nothing
  else about the business. It keeps its place in the steps it was in,
  its follow-up day stays as it was and it stays on the call sheet, as
  a business that didn't pick up does.
- **It took the call.** The record shows "Took a call on" and the day,
  on the call sheet and on its own page. Its history says it took
  the call. A business that asked to be called back or was interested
  took the call too, so it gets the same mark. A business that didn't
  pick up does not.
- **Talking counts as contact.** As before, a call where Simon spoke to
  the business counts as first contact, so its two years (decision
  0032) run from then.
- **Asked not to be contacted again.** This is a new outcome, "asked
  not to be contacted again" (`do_not_call` to the command). It marks
  the business do not contact, which can't be undone, so the web page
  checks with Simon first. That check used to be on "not interested".
- **Records already marked stay as they are.** Any business already
  marked do not contact by a "not interested" call keeps that mark.
  Nothing changes it by itself. Simon will look at those and decide.

## Consequences

- A polite "not interested" on the phone no longer loses a business
  for good. Its next letter, email or call goes ahead as it would have.
- A business that said "not interested" stays on the call sheet, as one
  that didn't answer does. The sheet shows the last call and the "Took
  a call" mark beside it, so Simon can see it was a real conversation
  before calling again.
- One more thing for Simon to judge on each call: was that "not
  interested" or "don't contact me again"? When it isn't clear, do not
  contact is the safe answer, as decision 0031 says for replies.
- Do not contact still can't be undone. That hasn't changed.

Simon accepted this on 21 November 2026.
